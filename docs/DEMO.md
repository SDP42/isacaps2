# Five-minute demo script

Press **Present** in the header (or ⌘K → *Start the demo walkthrough*). The step bar shows what to say and deep-links
each screen; Alt+→ / Alt+← moves between steps. Case steps open the most recent HIGH case (Scenario A if it exists),
so run **Scenario Lab → A · CFO voice note** once before the demo.

| Time | Step | Screen | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook | Landing | Move the cursor over the particle face, scroll one section | "Arup lost US$25 million after one video call where everyone else was synthetic. Seeing is no longer believing." |
| 0:25 | 1 · Forge the attack | Red-team lab | Pick *Executive voice-note wire fraud*, channel *WhatsApp*, **Forge attack and test MIRAGE**; show the 3D terrain | "We think like the attacker: a neural voice, laundered the way scammers send it. 40 of 42 laundered voice attacks caught; noise is the honest weak spot." |
| 1:05 | 2 · Is the voice real? | Voice check | **CFO voice note** → **Check this voice** | "Two independent anti-spoofing models agree it is synthetic. Then we read what it wants: a payment, secrecy, a deadline. AI voice plus a sensitive ask: voice-clone scam." |
| 1:45 | 3 · Full investigation | Case → Evidence | Point at the score waterfall, toggle *Media is authentic* in What-if | "Even if the voice were real, the request alone keeps this HIGH. A detector can never talk a payment down." |
| 2:20 | 4 · Why, with evidence | Case → Tribunal | Hover the Skeptic | "Every statement cites an exhibit. The Skeptic argues the innocent explanation; the Judge cannot change the score." |
| 2:45 | 5 · The cure | Case → Respond | **Run** the payment hold, tap *Already paid?* (12 minutes ago), open the cybercrime complaint, **Apply** the blocklist | "Detection ends in a response run: the hold drops exposure, the golden-hour clock starts, every complaint is pre-filled from the evidence, and the scammer's account is blocked for everyone." |
| 3:45 | 6 · Live on a call | Live call shield | **"CFO" asks for a wire** | "The same checks every three seconds, in the app or the Chrome extension. At the first HIGH moment, the plan slides in." |
| 4:25 | 7 · Why trust it | Models & audit | Show published vs measured numbers | "Published detectors, measured by us, with their weak spots shown. That is why you can act on the verdict." |
| 4:50 | Close | Case → Respond → Immunise | **Open drill** | "And the attack that got through becomes the drill everyone practises against." |
