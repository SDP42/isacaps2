# Limitations, ethics and the recommended response workflow

## Limitations

1. **Synthetic training data.** Media witnesses are trained on simulators, so their proxy metrics overstate real-world
   accuracy. Treat media scores as evidence that needs corroboration.
2. **Detectors lose the arms race.** New generators, languages and codecs degrade detectors. MIRAGE compensates with
   context (Trustbook, beneficiaries, policy) and an honest Inconclusive band, but cannot promise detection.
3. **Weak voiceprints.** The lightweight speaker embedding has a high EER; a mismatch is a weak signal.
4. **Lip-sync and blink cues can be faked or inherited** from a driving video; they are low-weight signals.
5. **No identity proof.** MIRAGE does not establish who is in a video. It checks whether the request fits the
   person the sender claims to be.
6. **Provenance gaps.** Most genuine images lack C2PA credentials; their absence is never counted against a file.
7. **Text model scope.** Hinglish coverage comes from templates; dialects and code-mixing beyond them will be weaker.
8. **Demo authentication.** Persona sign-in is for demonstration; production needs SSO, MFA and tenant isolation.

## Ethics

- Every demo sample is synthetic, generated by our own code, and labelled `MIRAGE SAMPLE`. No real public figure or
  private person was cloned.
- Voiceprints and style baselines are consent-based and revocable; raw media is content-addressed and can be purged
  while hashes and findings remain for the audit trail.
- The LLM never decides risk. It may only rephrase evidence-cited text, behind a guard that rejects new numbers or
  changed citations.
- MIRAGE shows its uncertainty (interval, Inconclusive) and mitigating evidence (the Skeptic) so it does not push
  users into false accusations.

## Recommended response workflow

```
Receive ─▶ Pause ─▶ Investigate in MIRAGE ─▶ Verify out of band ─▶ Decide ─▶ Report / Recover
```

1. **Pause.** Do not reply on the channel the request arrived on, and do not act on any deadline it sets.
2. **Investigate.** Drop every artefact into one case (voice note, screenshot, e-mail, number, UPI ID) with the
   claimed sender and channel.
3. **Read the verdict.** HIGH means do not act; MEDIUM means verify first; LOW means proceed with normal care;
   INCONCLUSIVE means verify anyway.
4. **Verify out of band** with the plan MIRAGE builds:
   - call the person on their **Trustbook / saved number**, never the number that contacted you;
   - ask the **family code word** or this week's **executive verification phrase**;
   - get a **second approver** for payments above the requester's limit;
   - verify **new beneficiary details** through the vendor master and a known contact;
   - **never share an OTP or password**, and use the official IT portal for resets.
5. **Decide and record.** Mark steps done; close the case as confirmed fraud (which flags the beneficiary for
   future cases), verified genuine, or already paid.
6. **Report and recover.** Export the incident report (hashes, exhibits, Tribunal record) and escalate to the SOC.
   If money moved: call **1930** within the hour, call the bank's fraud line, freeze UPI, and file at
   **cybercrime.gov.in**. Report organisational incidents to CERT-In.
