# Detection methodology and model cards

MIRAGE runs two kinds of models side by side: **public pretrained detectors** (trained on real deepfake data; the
primary layer, see [DETECTION.md](DETECTION.md)) and **its own explainable models** (trained here on synthetic data,
small, fast, feature-level explanations). Witnesses blend them and keep every score, so agreement or disagreement is
visible in the case view. This file documents MIRAGE's own models.

## Pretrained models

The pretrained detectors are now the **primary** layer of every media witness, and MIRAGE's own models below are the
supporting layer (10–15% in logit space plus the explanation features). Models, calibration, ensembling and our
benchmark are documented in [DETECTION.md](DETECTION.md).

| Component | Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Speech-to-text | faster-whisper `base` (int8) | Language detected; Hindi-family languages transcribed in Roman script with a Hinglish prompt; skipped under 2.5 s; low-confidence or repetitive output dropped |
| OCR | RapidOCR (ONNX) | Line text and boxes; flagged lines are drawn on the screenshot |

Pretrained models are optional (`MIRAGE_PRETRAINED=off`); every witness falls back to MIRAGE's own model.

# MIRAGE's own models

No dataset was provided with PS-02, and the strongest public deepfake corpora (ASVspoof 5, FaceForensics++,
Celeb-DF, IndicSynth) need licences and GPUs we did not have at build time. We therefore built **simulators** that
reproduce the documented fingerprints of each attack class, trained lightweight, explainable models on them, and
designed every witness so a production model can be swapped in behind the same interface. Metrics below are
regenerated by `python -m ml.train_all` into `backend/models_store/metrics.json` and shown live on the
**Models & audit** page.

**Read the numbers honestly.** Simulated data is easier than the real world. Proxy scores near 1.0 show the pipeline
works end to end; they say nothing about accuracy on real deepfakes. That is why MIRAGE never lets a single detector
decide: policy rules and identity context carry as much weight as media forensics.

## Words: scam-language classifier

- **Model:** char (2–5) + word (1–2) TF-IDF, logistic regression, sigmoid calibration (3-fold); plus a tactic lexicon
  (EN / Devanagari / Hinglish) returning character spans, and regex extractors for action, amount (Indian units),
  UPI, account, IFSC, IBAN, URL, phone, e-mail and deadline.
- **Data:** `ml/synth_text.py` generates about 3,000 messages from 10 scam families (CEO fraud, vendor BEC,
  credential, family emergency, digital arrest, KYC, job/reward) and 3 legitimate families that deliberately use
  money, urgency and authority words innocently.
- **Split:** `GroupShuffleSplit` by template, so the test phrasing was never seen in training.
- **Result:** AUC 0.94 on unseen templates, with AUC also reported per language. Calibration (ECE about 0.18) is the
  weakest point on unseen templates, so the score is combined with tactic evidence, never used alone.
- **Production path:** MuRIL / mDeBERTa fine-tuned on real phishing and SMS-spam corpora plus a reviewed Hinglish set.

## Voice: synthetic speech

- **Features:** jitter, F0 variation, shimmer, spectral flatness, high-band ratio, spectral flux and roll-off
  variability, MFCC-delta variance, silence ratio, vocoder frame periodicity, voiced ratio, 7–8 kHz floor.
- **Model:** isotonic-calibrated gradient boosting; 2 s windows (4 s for long clips), attention pooling, merged
  suspicious segments become waveform regions. Clips under 2 s go to Inconclusive.
- **Data:** `ml/synth_audio.py`, a source-filter simulator. Genuine clips have natural jitter, shimmer, breath and
  room reverb; synthetic clips have a quality parameter from crude vocoder to high-fidelity clone. Both pass through
  the same random channel (telephony band-pass, WhatsApp-style low-pass and quantisation, μ-law, noise).
- **Split:** held-out random speakers and channels, plus a μ-law-only channel report.
- **Result:** near-perfect on the proxy (μ-law AUC about 0.97). Not representative of real clones.
- **Channel profile:** codec cliffs at 3.4 kHz and 6–7.5 kHz feed the Skeptic's innocent-explanation argument.
- **Production path:** wav2vec2-XLSR + AASIST on ASVspoof 5, In-the-Wild and IndicSynth with codec augmentation;
  `MIRAGE_HF_AUDIO_MODEL` already averages a Hugging Face checkpoint into the window scores.

## Voiceprint: speaker verification

- Background-normalised embedding (median pitch plus long-term mel spectrum), cosine score, threshold set at the
  measured equal-error point.
- **Result:** EER about 0.24 on held-out simulated speakers, which is **weak**. A mismatch is therefore severity 2,
  and the fusion engine discounts it. Production path: ECAPA-TDNN (SpeechBrain) with consented enrolment.

## Provenance: AI-generated images

- **Features:** ELA mean, std and block variation; noise-residual level and uniformity; radial spectrum slope;
  upsampling peak strength; colour-filter-array correlation; saturation; gradient kurtosis.
- **Model:** isotonic-calibrated gradient boosting; plus metadata checks (generator tags, PNG parameters, EXIF
  camera, editing software) and C2PA manifest presence. Missing C2PA is explicitly *not* evidence.
- **Data:** `ml/synth_images.py`: camera-like (1/f scenes, Bayer mosaic and demosaic, shot and read noise, JPEG)
  vs generator-like (half-resolution render, zero-insertion upsampling, near-zero noise), randomly re-saved.
- **Result:** AUC about 0.997 on held-out seeds (proxy). Production path: CLIP ViT-L/14 linear probe (UnivFD) on GenImage.

## Face: video

- Haar face tracking on 8–48 sampled frames; frame scores from the pixel-forensics model on face crops; 3-frame
  smoothing and attention pooling; track jitter; **lip-sync** as the lag that maximises correlation between
  mouth-region motion energy and the speech envelope (±500 ms). Offsets over 120 ms are flagged.
- Production path: a FaceForensics++ / Celeb-DF trained network (GenConViT or EfficientNet-B4) and SyncNet.

## Identity: request anomaly

- Isolation Forest over channel-usual, out-of-hours distance, log amount, amount / approval limit, beneficiary
  recency and action class, fitted on 12 months of simulated normal traffic and scored on injected attacks
  (AUC about 0.997).

## Fusion

```
A = 1 − Π(1 − sᵢ·(0.35 + 0.65·confᵢ))         media witnesses, voiceprint mismatch boosts voice
M = words score     C = identity score     S = 0.1 none · 0.3 info · 0.6 data · 1.0 money/credential
raw  = 1 − (1 − A·S)(1 − M·S)(1 − C)
risk = 100 · σ(logit(raw) / T),  T fitted by temperature scaling (ml/calibrate_fusion.py)
```

Policy rules (credential request → HIGH; money plus secrecy, bypass or an unverified sender → HIGH; likely synthetic
plus a sensitive ask → HIGH; synthetic with no ask and no tactics → LOW with a `SYNTHETIC_MEDIA` tag; large amount to
a new beneficiary → at least MEDIUM; no usable evidence → INCONCLUSIVE) run before the band is set. The uncertainty
interval perturbs each lens by its witness's confidence. Per-lens contributions are exact Shapley values of `raw`,
scaled to the final risk.
