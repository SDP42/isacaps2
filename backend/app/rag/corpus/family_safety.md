title: Family safety: code words and what to do after paying
source: MIRAGE consumer guidance, based on I4C and bank awareness material (paraphrased)
kind: guidance

# Agree a code word
Choose a word or phrase only your family knows, agreed in person. Ask for it whenever a call, voice note or video asks for money or urgent help, even if the voice sounds exactly right.

# Call back on the saved number
Hang up and call the person on the number already saved in your phone. Scammers often claim the phone is broken or that they are using a friend's phone.

# If you already paid
Call 1930 at once; reports in the first hour have the best chance of freezing the money. Call your bank's fraud line, block the beneficiary and freeze UPI. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with screenshots and the MIRAGE report. Do not delete messages. Tell someone you trust; scams depend on secrecy.
