# T1566 Phishing
Adversaries send messages to trick users into revealing information or performing actions. Sub-techniques include spearphishing attachments, links and spearphishing via service, which covers messages delivered through social media, chat apps and personal email rather than corporate mail.

# T1656 Impersonation
Adversaries impersonate a trusted person or organisation to persuade a target to act, for example by posing as an executive, vendor or IT staff. Impersonation is commonly combined with urgency and authority to get payments approved or credentials disclosed. Business email compromise and vendor invoice fraud are typical outcomes.

# T1598 Phishing for Information
Messages designed to elicit sensitive information such as credentials, MFA codes or internal details, often by pretending to be IT support or a service provider.

# Mitigations
User training focused on verification behaviour, out-of-band confirmation for financial transactions, restricting who can change beneficiary details, and monitoring for impersonation of executives across channels.
