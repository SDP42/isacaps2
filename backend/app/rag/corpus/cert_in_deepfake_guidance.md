# Threat overview
CERT-In describes deepfakes as AI-generated or AI-altered audio, video and images that convincingly imitate real people. The advisory highlights financial fraud through impersonation of executives and family members, disinformation, reputational harm, and the use of synthetic media to defeat identity checks.

# How attacks are delivered
Attackers combine synthetic media with familiar social engineering: a cloned voice call or voice note, a fake video meeting, or an AI-generated profile photo, supported by messages that create urgency and secrecy. Requests usually ask for a transfer of money, sharing of credentials or OTPs, or sensitive data.

# Recommended measures for organisations
Deploy AI-based detection tools and digital forensics capability. Establish verification procedures for high-value or unusual requests, such as call-backs on known numbers and multi-person approval. Train employees to verify rather than trust what they see or hear. Maintain an incident response and crisis communication plan that covers deepfake incidents. Preserve evidence and report incidents to CERT-In.

# Recommended measures for individuals
Be cautious with unexpected calls or videos that ask for money or personal information, even from familiar voices or faces. Verify through a separate trusted channel. Agree a code word with family members. Report cyber fraud to the national helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.
