# Approval matrix
Accounts Payable may release up to ₹5,00,000 per payment. The Financial Controller may approve up to ₹25,00,000. The CFO may approve up to ₹1,00,00,000. Any payment above the approver's limit requires a second approver from the next level. Payments above ₹10,00,000 to a beneficiary first seen within 30 days always require two approvers.

# New or changed beneficiary
Changes to vendor bank details must be raised through the vendor master workflow and confirmed by calling the vendor on the number already on file, never a number given in the change request. Payments to a new beneficiary are held for 24 hours.

# Call-back rule
Any payment request that arrives by WhatsApp, SMS, personal email, phone or video call must be confirmed by calling the requester on their directory number. Executives never request payments over WhatsApp. A request that asks the employee to keep it secret from audit or finance is treated as fraudulent until verified.

# Rotating verification phrase
The CEO, CFO and MD receive a weekly verification phrase through the security team. Staff may ask for it before acting on any unusual instruction.
